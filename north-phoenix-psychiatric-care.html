One office, broader North Valley access

The office is in Anthem—not a collection of city-specific locations.

Back to Life Mental Health is an independent psychiatric practice located at 41810 N Venture Dr, Building C, Suite C122, in Anthem, Arizona. The office is accessible to patients traveling from North Phoenix and nearby North Valley communities. The practice does not claim separate offices in North Phoenix, Norterra, New River, Cave Creek, or the other communities listed here.

For patients who prefer virtual care or live farther away, telehealth may be available when the patient is physically located in Arizona at the time of the appointment and telehealth is clinically appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about North Valley access

Do you have an office in North Phoenix?

No. The physical office is in Anthem at 41810 N Venture Dr, Building C, Suite C122. The practice serves patients who travel from North Phoenix and surrounding North Valley communities.

Can I use telehealth instead of driving to Anthem?

Telehealth is available for patients located in Arizona when it is clinically appropriate. Some situations may still be better suited to in-person care or additional medical evaluation.

Do you see teenagers as well as adults?

Yes. Back to Life Mental Health provides psychiatric care for adolescents and adults.

Does scheduling an evaluation guarantee a prescription?

No. Medication may be one option after evaluation, but prescribing depends on the clinical picture, safety, medical factors, treatment history, and whether medication is appropriate.
