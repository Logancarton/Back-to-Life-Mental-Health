Serving the North Valley

Anthem office access for North Phoenix and surrounding communities.

The physical office is in Anthem and serves patients from North Phoenix and nearby North Valley communities, including Norterra, Tramonto, North Gateway, Desert Hills, New River, Cave Creek, and Carefree. Back to Life Mental Health does not claim separate offices in those communities.

Telehealth provides an option for established and new patients located elsewhere in Arizona when clinically appropriate.
