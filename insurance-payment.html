Before your first visit

Three useful questions for your insurer.

  • Is Back to Life Mental Health in network for my specific plan?
  • What is my outpatient behavioral-health copay, coinsurance, or deductible?
  • Do psychiatric evaluation or medication-management visits require authorization?
Why verify?

Your card does not tell the whole story.

Two people with the same insurance company can have very different benefits. Employer, marketplace, and individual plans may use different networks and cost-sharing rules.

Benefit information provided before a visit should be treated as an estimate until the insurer processes the claim.

A practical note

Insurance coverage and clinical care are separate questions.

A plan can determine what it will pay for, but treatment decisions are based on the clinical evaluation, safety, and what is appropriate for you. If coverage limits an option, the next step is to talk through alternatives rather than assume care stops there.
