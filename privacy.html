Website privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains how the public Back to Life Mental Health website handles information and how external services may be involved when you choose to contact or schedule with the practice.

Website information

This public website provides general information about Back to Life Mental Health LLC, its services, and ways to contact or schedule with the practice. The public website should not be used to send urgent, emergency, or highly sensitive clinical information.

Scheduling and third-party services

Online scheduling links may open a third-party service used by the practice. Information submitted through that service is governed by the service's privacy and security practices as well as any agreements applicable to the practice.

Email

Email is provided as a convenient contact method for routine administrative communication. Standard email may not be an appropriate channel for sensitive health information or urgent clinical concerns.

Analytics, cookies, and future features

The current custom website is designed to function without requiring a patient account. If analytics, forms, patient tools, or other interactive features are added later, this policy should be updated to describe what information is collected, why it is collected, and how it is handled.

External links

The site may link to scheduling, maps, insurance, educational, or other external resources. Back to Life Mental Health does not control the privacy practices of unrelated external websites.

Questions

For questions about this website or privacy practices, contact Admin@BTLMH.com or (480) 313-8583.

This website privacy notice describes the public website. Patient privacy, medical records, and protected health information may be governed by additional practice notices, forms, and applicable law.
