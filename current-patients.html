Secure messaging

How to message your provider through Tebra.

Secure messages stay within the Patient Portal instead of traveling through standard email or text messaging.

01 / Sign in

Open your portal

Go to the Tebra Patient Portal and sign in with your account email and password.

02 / Messages

Open Messages

From the portal dashboard, choose View Messages to see your inbox and sent messages.

03 / Compose

Choose New Message

Select the provider, add a subject, and write your message. Tebra also allows an image or document attachment up to 10 MB.

04 / Send

Send Secure Message

Review the message and choose Send Secure Message. It is delivered to the provider's Tebra Message Center.

Portal access

Password help without sharing passwords.

Back to Life Mental Health does not collect, store, see, or retrieve your Tebra Patient Portal password.

Forgot your password?

Reset it directly through Tebra.

  1. Open the Tebra Patient Portal.
  2. Select Forgot your password?
  3. Enter the email address associated with your portal account.
  4. Follow the password-reset email from Tebra.

Never activated your account?

Ask the office to resend your invitation.

Patient Portal access begins with an invitation from the practice. In the invitation, choose Get Started. Tebra opens the account setup with the invited email address already filled in, then asks the patient to create a password and enter their date of birth.

If you never received the invitation or need it sent again, call Back to Life Mental Health and we can review your portal status and resend the invitation when appropriate.

For privacy, account access issues involving an already activated email may require assistance through Tebra rather than changing credentials on this public website.

Inside your portal

More than a message inbox.

Available portal features can vary by account and practice settings, but Tebra may provide access to the following tools.

Health records

Review available medications, problems, allergies, vitals, lab information, and other health-record information shared through Tebra.

Documents

Review documents shared by the practice, including applicable treatment plans, patient education, and uploaded documents.

Billing & receipts

If those features are enabled for your account, Tebra can display bills, portal payments, and applicable itemized receipts.

Authorized guests

Patients can use Tebra's guest-access tools to authorize another person, such as a family member, to access permitted information without sharing the patient's password.

Use the right channel

Keep clinical information in the secure portal.

For medication questions, treatment information, or sensitive documents, use Tebra secure messaging. Ordinary email and standard text messaging should not be used to send protected health information. For scheduling questions, call the office or use online scheduling.

The Patient Portal is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or have an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
